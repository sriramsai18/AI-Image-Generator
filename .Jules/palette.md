## 2024-08-04 - High-Contrast Focus Indicators in Gradio Dark Themes
**Learning:** In custom-themed dark Gradio applications, default focus outlines often disappear or blend in, and Gradio frequently applies `outline: none !important` to inputs. Standard CSS focus styling fails to override these. To guarantee keyboard-only navigation accessibility, high-contrast `:focus-visible` styles must use high-specificity selectors (e.g., prefixing with `.gradio-container`) and use `!important` on `outline` and `box-shadow` properties to ensure focus indicators are properly displayed.
**Action:** Always use high-specificity selectors and `!important` for `:focus-visible` styles on interactive elements like buttons, inputs, textareas, and links when designing custom themes for Gradio apps.
