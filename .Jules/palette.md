## 2026-07-27 - High-contrast Accessibility Focus Indicators on Custom Dark Cyberpunk Themes in Gradio
**Learning:** When using custom stylesheets in Gradio to implement a dark, neon, or cyberpunk look (like red neon or green matrices), default browser outline rings (which are often blue or dark grey) can blend into the dark background or contrast poorly. Explicitly declaring high-contrast `:focus-visible` styles with a bright primary hue (such as amber `#ffcc00`) and outline offset ensures keyboard-only/screen-reader users can clearly identify the focused active control.
**Action:** Always declare a universal or element-specific `:focus-visible` style rule with `outline-offset` and clear contrasting neon colors (e.g., `#ffcc00` or `#39ff14`) when crafting custom dark/neon stylesheets.
