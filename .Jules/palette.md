## 2025-08-09 - High-Contrast Keyboard Focus Indicators in Cyberpunk Gradio Theme
**Learning:** In highly customized Gradio stylesheets (e.g., dark, neon, or cyberpunk themes), standard keyboard focus indicators are completely suppressed or masked by layout/input overrides. Standard focus CSS rules fail because Gradio's internal classes inject explicit boundaries and inline styles with `!important` markers. To reliably ensure focus visibility for screen readers and keyboard-only users, higher-specificity global focus declarations (like `.gradio-container *:focus-visible`) must be added using `!important` on the custom outline and offset styles.
**Action:** Always add `.gradio-container *:focus-visible` custom rules with `!important` outlines and shadows to restore high-contrast focus rings when styling custom Gradio themes.
